About ▾

Every stroke is generated by a painting engine: the photograph is simplified into colour masses, a structure tensor decides which way the brush runs, and the picture is built coarse-to-fine, one bristled stroke at a time. The accumulated paint height is then lit as an impasto relief.

Sliders update the After image live: it is drawn on the graphics card, from the same planned strokes that Run paints in full quality.
